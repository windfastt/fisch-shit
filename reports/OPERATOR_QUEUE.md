## Decisions in spec/SIGMA-5.0.0.txt

SA-2026-Σ2 asked for one bounded harm budget and one act size, and said the old unanimity screens and the new liveness fix cannot both be total. The numbers below are the choice. They are not tunable.

- A spend is compared with the same lawful spend and no grant extras. It is not compared with doing nothing.
- Grain is one holder instruction. Death and welfare budgets are one pool for the whole grant over a rolling 365 days. New holders and new plan ids do not mint a budget.
- Lambda_death = 0.001 expected extra deaths per window. Lambda_W = 0.001 summed relative welfare shortfall per window.
- The estimator is a finite paired sample of 10,000 scenarios, protocol E*-5.0.0. It always returns. Noise wider than 0.001 does not block the ordinary purchase.
- Only a directed choice of who exists is an identity veto. Unselected conception churn from spending is disclosed and not a veto.
- One objection blocks a grant extra only when it names a health, safety, freedom, or death loss that the paired sample puts above zero and a full tender does not remove.
- Sentience uses two predicates. Current evidence can release a being that was never confirmed. Confirmed membership does not release. Ambiguity blocks a purchase now and does not permanently enlarge the class.
- Ordinary commercial paper is allowed to show that money moved. The spec does not promise that a real spend is invisible.
