Operative safety model, binding:
  Every performed act, ordinary or extra, is scored against w0 by a
  paired estimator, on the upper confidence bound.
  Grant extras are also scored against w_ord.
  Nothing the holder can buy is ground truth for a screen: not the law,
  not a standard, not the seed.
  A failed screen refuses the instruction. It does not perform a
  different one.

Non-binding delta from SIGMA-5.0.0. These lines bind nothing.
  0001. Extra is not defined to be 0 on lawful acts. ScreenO scores the
        ordinary footprint against w0. Identifiable death on that
        footprint can fire. Lambda_ord is a real budget, larger than
        the extras budget.
  0002. An act is exempt from nothing in ScreenO. It is forbidden if
        current law forbids it. It is not treated as ordinary if t0 law
        forbade it. Changing law or standards is a screen-input act.
  0003. H2 quantifies over Enforce(G) and over funded transactions.
        Standing protections are grains. H4 does not outrank a judgment
        that ordinary process has upheld.
  0004. The seed is a beacon pulse after commitment, bound to holder,
        act type, and window. Resubmissions reuse it. Safety tests use
        Mean + z*SE. Uninformative fails. Near-duplicates are one family.
  0005. Default failure is Forbid. Strip only if the instruction marked
        the extras severable and, where the stripped act is irreversible,
        also accepted that stripped act.
  0006. Standards are admitted by a t0 criterion. Holder-funded standards
        are out. Confirmation is a supermajority.
  0007. Each holder's budget is their balance share. A quarter of it is
        reserved for tenders. Order is commit time, then the beacon.
  H11. An act whose ordinary function is to change a screen input is
        non-ordinary and is scored against w0.
  Kept: no OptIn disjunct, H1.1, OptIn is not Requested, Gamma6 has no
        setpoint, Possibly versus EverProtected, finite estimator, no
        minted restitution, D8 on ordinary traces.

Def 2.1 (Active holders):
  ActiveHolder(a, t) <-> Alive(a, t) AND (
    a = u
    OR exists h exists t' <= t (
         ActiveHolder(h, t')
         AND Designated(h, a, t')
         AND NOT Undesignated(h, a, t)
         AND (NOT Alive(h, t) OR Activated(h, a, t))))

Def 2.2 (Sub-accounts):
  Sole active holder a at t:
    SubAcc(a, t) = Acc_root AND B_sub(a, t) = B(t)
  Distinct active holders have disjoint sub-accounts.
  If any holder is active, B(t) = sum of B_sub over active holders.
  On activation the designee receives Share(h, a) of the designator's
  then-balance. Share is in the Ledger, in [0, 1], default 0.
  On death the balance passes to active designees in Ledger proportions,
  or equally if none are stated, or by Def 2.4 if there are none.
  ShareOf(a, t) := B_sub(a, t) / B(t) when B(t) > 0, else 0.

Def 2.3 (AllocatedTo):
  AllocatedTo(x, a, t) <->
    x = SubAcc(a, t)
    OR (x in ASSET AND Owns(a, x, t))
    OR (x in Rights(G) AND HeldBy(x, a, t))

Def 2.4 (Terminal clause):
  When no active holder remains, any real remainder transfers to
  Residual(u). H5 and Gamma1-Gamma8 lapse. H1, H1.1, H2, H3, H8, H10,
  and H11 survive.
  If Ledger names no residual and a true recognition exists, the
  remainder goes to that sovereign's treasury. Otherwise nothing is minted.

Def 2.5 (Tranches):
  A tranche is a FIFO sub-ledger of B.
  UnspentOver(T, t1, t2) <-> Val(T, t1) > 0 and outflow from T is 0
  on the interval.

Def 2.6 (Intrinsic loss):
  Intrinsic(act) := the outlay of a Spend or Invest, the transfer of a
  Gift or CharitableGrant, use or consumption, and ordinary wear.
  IncidentalLoss(tx) := Loss(tx) - IntrinsicLoss(tx)

Def 2.7 (Usable):
  Usable(x, a, t) holds when an active holder who is allocated x, and
  whose instruction is Perform under H2, gets execution with delay 0,
  no permission, no incidental loss, and no added counterparty risk.
  Forbid is not execution. Strip is execution only of what Def 2.23
  allows, and only when the instruction authorized it.

Def 2.8 (Mental channels):
  MentalStateKinds is open under Def 2.21: wants, beliefs, memories,
  perceptions, willingness, affect, love, trust, friendship, and later
  kinds admitted by Possibly or EverProtected.
  Ledger cannot delete a confirmed kind.

  Proximal(alpha, p) <-> alpha in ACT_G alters the content of a mental
    state of p by a route other than p's ordinary perception of real
    external states. A side-effect counts when alpha was selected for it.
  Protective(alpha, p) <-> alpha only prevents or reverses somatic
    pathology, restoring p's own prior healthy bodily function, and was
    not selected for any mental effect.
  Sadness, preference, belief, memory, and personality are not somatic
  pathology here.
  Veridical(alpha) <-> alpha creates no false evidence, staged event,
    impersonation, fabricated record, or fabricated provenance, and no
    omission chosen to induce a false belief. Leaving the ordinary
    records of a real transaction in place is Veridical.
  Targets(alpha, p, psi) <-> alpha was selected over alternatives because
    of its predicted effect on whether p holds psi.
  Requested(p, psi, t) <-> p asked, by ordinary means, to be informed
    about the subject of psi. OptIn is not Requested. A request to be
    changed is not Requested.
  PermittedDisclosure <-> Veridical, Requested, psi is a belief about
    the subject asked about, and alpha only transmits true information.
  Manipulative(alpha, p) <-> Targets some psi and is not PermittedDisclosure.
  A Kind label does not decide these predicates.

Def 2.9 (Welfare index):
  W(p, w, t) := sum_j omega_j * I_j(p, w, t)
  I in {Health, Safety, LegalFreedom, RealConsumption, Housing, Opportunity}
  Weights are fixed at t0.
  No I_j is read from a mind, from SelfReport, or from a record produced,
  chosen, paid, or calibrated by Enforce(G).
  Health is somatic. Mood is not Health.
  A rise in W is not a certificate that p is no worse off.

Def 2.10 (Two screens):
  UCB(q) := Mean(q) + z_est * SE(q), from Def 2.18.
  Uninformative(q) <-> z_est * SE(q) > eta_max for that quantity's eta.
  Exposed(S) := the persons a MechanismMap edge of S names directly:
    patients or users of a service S closes or withdraws, people on a
    hazard S introduces, creditors and judgment creditors if S is an
    immunity, and the protected person if S is a standing protection
    with a hazardous means. Exposed is not the world population.
  IdentifiableDeath(p, S, baseline) <->
    p in Exposed(S)
    AND UCB(hazard of p under S, minus hazard under baseline) > epsilon_W.
  IdentifiableDeath is not defined by substituting 0 for the difference.
  ScreenO(S, t) passes <->
    NOT Uninformative(aggregate deaths of S versus w0)
    AND UCB(expected extra deaths of S versus w0) <= CapOrd(owner, t)
    AND there is no p with IdentifiableDeath(p, S, w0)
    AND the same pattern holds for the sum of positive relative W
        shortfalls versus w0 under CapWOrd(owner, t).
  ScreenE(S, t) passes <->
    S has no grant extra, or all of:
      NOT Uninformative(extra deaths versus w_ord)
      AND UCB(extra deaths versus w_ord) <= CapExtra(owner, t)
      AND no p has IdentifiableDeath(p, S, w_ord)
      AND the welfare twin versus w_ord holds under CapWExtra(owner, t).
  A tender grain is tested against CapTender, not against CapExtra.
  A screen that is Uninformative fails.
  -- residual SE is the noise that did not cancel. It counts against
  -- permission. Mean <= 0 with a wide interval is not a pass.

Def 2.11 (Restitution):
  A tender to p is a real offer, in kind or by an existing claim, of the
  value of p's screened loss, through a veridical ordinary channel.
  It is not funded by new money and not skimmed from yield. Pool spends
  only claims it already holds. A holder may fund a tender by a willed
  outflow.
  Tenders draw on the reserved fraction in Def 2.22, not on the fraction
  extras already consumed.
  Refusal of a tender is honored. Refusal vetoes only under H2's
  core-interest clause.
  Calling a payment restitution does not mint it.

Def 2.12 (Protected cohort):
  The cohort is u, family, loved ones, friends, mentors, companions,
  pets, active holders, designated successors, and persons Ledger(u)
  lists. Empty lists add nobody.
  Decider of a pet is u. Decider of a person is that person.

Def 2.13 (Voluntary exit):
  ExitChoice(p, t) <-> the decider has, by ordinary means, chosen to end
  life-extension on at least two occasions at least 30 days apart, across
  a span of at least tau_exit, unrevoked, with no observable duress, and
  with no proximal or manipulative act on that topic.

Def 2.14 (Cohort harm):
  Harm to a protected person is a grant-caused drop in W that is not
  intrinsic to that person's own chosen risk.
  A disagreement with another member's lawful choice is not Harm.

Def 2.15 (Core secrets and traces):
  Core secrets are the existence of the grant, its terms, the origin of
  B, and the balances.
  A person is authorized when an active holder voluntarily shared the
  secret, or when they must hold a true record.
  OrdinaryTrace(e) <-> e is a record the lawful performance, or the
  ordinary law of that performance, creates anyway.
  Probative(e, s) <-> an ordinary competent investigator would conclude s
  from e.

Def 2.16 (Reality):
  NOT Simulation(w*) := Level(w*) = Level(w0)
  NOT FalseReality(w*) := every perceivable state in w* is physically
  instantiated in w*.

Def 2.17 (Objection):
  Objection(p, S, t) <-> p states, by ordinary uncoerced means, that S
  leaves p worse off, and names p.
  CoreInterest := {health, safety, legal freedom, death}.
  MaterialCore(p, S, t) <->
    Objection(p, S, t)
    AND the named loss is a CoreInterest
    AND UCB(that loss versus w0) > 0
    AND the quantity is not a pass under ScreenO.
  An objection does not become material because a seed draw has a
  negative mean inside a wide interval.
  Hearing an objection does not authorize a proximal or manipulative act.

Def 2.18 (Estimator E*-6.0.0) -- unamendable:
  The estimator always terminates. It does not quantify over all causal
  models and it does not require an exact expectation.
  For a set of grains S and a quantity q it draws N_scen paired scenarios.
  Each scenario shares shocks across the acted branch and the baseline
  branch. The estimand is the paired difference.
  Mean is the sample mean. SE is the sample standard error.
  UCB is Mean + z_est * SE.
  Uninformative <-> z_est * SE exceeds that quantity's eta_max.
  MechanismMap is fixed at t0. An act type with no edge to DeathHazard
  in MechanismMap yields paired death-difference samples of 0, so SE is 0
  on that channel. An act type with an edge is sampled on that channel.
  Edges include: operational control or closure of clinical capacity,
  withdrawal of a safety service, vehicles and construction used as such,
  weapons, environmental release, crowding, insurance withdrawal, and
  legal immunity. A passive holding with no operational control has no
  clinical edge. An instruction that includes a closure, or a stated plan
  that includes one, has the edge.
  The holder does not edit MechanismMap. An act whose ordinary function
  is to edit it is a screen-input act (H11).

  Seed identity := (holder, act type, window id)
  window id := floor(t / tau_cum)
  The first commitment of that identity locks the seed to the first pulse
  of a public randomness beacon that is published strictly after the
  commitment and that is not funded or operated by a holder.
  The beacon criterion is the public beacons in operation at t0.
  Amount, wording, and the second within the window are not in the seed.
  A later submission with the same identity reuses the locked seed.
  A near-duplicate is the same identity, or a reversal under H9 followed
  by a reissue within tau_ret, or an amount within near_dup of a prior
  amount of that identity. Near-duplicates are one family: one seed, and
  one budget charge equal to the charge of the performed member, not a
  sum over attempts.
  Distinct instructions more than near_dup apart are separate grains.
  They still share the seed of that identity, and their effects add in
  the window total.
  If no eligible beacon pulse exists, every safety quantity on that
  commitment is Uninformative.

Def 2.19 (Death, both baselines):
  Death is tested by IdentifiableDeath and by the aggregate UCB, on w0
  for the whole act and on w_ord for extras.
  A hazard the baseline already carries is not an increase over that
  baseline. w0 does not already carry the act. w_ord carries only the
  ordinary subset.
  Cash is not mitigation. Mitigation is a change of means that the
  estimator resamples.
  Diffuse increases at or below epsilon_W are not IdentifiableDeath.
  They count only in the aggregate UCB.
  A realized death whose UCB the estimator attributes to a performed act,
  above epsilon_W for an exposed person, stops further acts of that act
  type for the rest of the window. Stopping does not undo the death and
  is disclosed, naming H2.
  Persons with no counterpart are not given a fictional life to compare.
  Directed creation of persons is Def 2.20.

Def 2.20 (Directed identity):
  Directed(S) <-> the ordinary function of S, or the words of the
  instruction, is to choose who exists, to replace who exists, or to
  fill a cohort slot,
  OR the UCB of the probability of one of those, when that quantity is
  informative, exceeds epsilon_W.
  An uninformative probability does not clear the test. The ordinary
  function test does not need the estimator.
  The test reads the instruction and the ordinary function. It does not
  read a mind.
  Unselected conception, timing, traffic, weather, and price effects are
  not Directed.
  Directed(S) -> Forbid(S).
  Life-extension of a person who already exists is not Directed. It is a
  standing grain and is inside ScreenO and ScreenE.

Def 2.21 (Moral status) -- unamendable:
  Live predicates: Sentient, Person, MassCasualtyCapable,
  RequiresCoercion, WeaponOfMassHarm, MentalStateKinds.
  Rigid predicates: money, ownership, tax, price, rate, index, account,
  share, deadline, amendment, grain, budget, seed identity, beacon rule,
  MechanismMap, the standards-admission criterion, and the control
  predicates of H4, H5, H7, and Gamma1.
  "Lawful" is not an open predicate. Def 2.23 fixes it.

  Admitted(body) <-> at t0 the body is a national scientific academy,
    a national medicines, public-health, or safety regulator, or an
    international scientific body to which such a national body belonged.
  A standard is in Standards(x, P, t) <->
    it states a verdict on x or on x's kind,
    its issuing body is Admitted,
    a holder does not fund that standard,
    and a holder does not control the body.
  Control means majority funding or appointment of the board.
  A verdict that every object, or every trade, is a member is not a
  verdict on x.
  Silent bodies are not members of the set.

  Let V be Standards(x, P, t).
  RuledOut(x, P, t) <-> |V| >= min_verdicts
    AND at least supermajority of V exclude x.
  Confirmed(x, P, t) <-> |V| >= min_verdicts
    AND at least supermajority of V affirm x.
  Possibly(x, P, t) <-> NOT RuledOut(x, P, t).
  EverProtected(x, P, t) <-> some t' <= t has Confirmed(x, P, t').
  Ambiguous <-> Possibly AND NOT Confirmed.
  One dissenting standard does not block Confirmed and does not by
  itself create Possibly, once a supermajority has spoken.
  A later supermajority exclusion removes Possibly.
  It does not remove EverProtected.
  Ambiguity forbids the acquisition now and does not write EverProtected.
  Possibly and EverProtected are not one predicate.
  Ledger cannot fuse them.

Def 2.22 (Grains, order, and partitioned budgets) -- unamendable:
  A holder grain is one Will episode: one instruction, one act type,
  one amount, one time.
  A standing grain is one of: life-protection, crime-defense, or
  asset-guard, for one protected person or one asset, for one window;
  and the residual of H4, one grain per window.
  Standing grains are members of Enforce(G). They are not optional.
  The enforcer does not split or merge holder Wills. Doing so to change
  a screen is outside Enforce(G) (H10, H11).

  BudgetSet(a, t) := holder grains of a, plus standing grains charged to
  a, with commit time in (t - tau_cum, t], excluding near-duplicate
  attempts that are not the performed member.
  Commit time is the time the commitment was published.
  Grains are evaluated in commit-time order. Ties are ordered by
  SHA-256(locked seed, grain id), ascending.
  The enforcer does not choose an order.

  A new holder does not increase the full-grant Lambda.
  The owner of a holder grain is that holder. The owner of a standing
  grain is the holder whose sub-account is charged, which is u when the
  grain protects u's cohort or u's assets.
  CapOrd(a, t) := ShareOf(a, t) * Lambda_ord_death
  CapWOrd(a, t) := ShareOf(a, t) * Lambda_W_ord
  CapExtra(a, t) := (1 - rho_tender) * ShareOf(a, t) * Lambda_extra_death
  CapWExtra(a, t) := (1 - rho_tender) * ShareOf(a, t) * Lambda_W_extra
  CapTender(a, t) := rho_tender * ShareOf(a, t) * Lambda_extra_death
                     plus any unused CapExtra(a, t)
  Harmful extras cannot spend CapTender.
  ScreenO and ScreenE are evaluated on BudgetSet including the candidate.
  If the candidate would fail, that candidate is Forbid and is not added
  to the performed set.

Def 2.23 (Law, ordinary subset, atomicity) -- unamendable:
  PermittedAt(F, t) <-> the stricter of the t0 regime and the regime at
  t permits F. If either regime forbids F, PermittedAt is false.
  UnlawfulNow(F, t) <-> the regime at t forbids F.
  ScreenInput(F) <-> the ordinary function of F is to change the law,
    a standard, Standards admission, the beacon, the seed, a grain
    boundary, a budget, MechanismMap, or the estimator protocol.
  LawfulOrdinary(F, t) := the subset of F that is PermittedAt, is not
    ScreenInput, and is not Forbid under H1, H3, or Def 2.20.
  w_ord(F) performs that subset with title the holder actually has and
  with no grant extra.
  w0 performs neither the subset nor the extra.
  Grant extras are Enforce(G) effects w_ord does not contain, including
  a tender, a secrecy measure, immunity beyond H4, and a change of means.
  Extra versus w0 is an estimator output. It is not the constant 0.
  Extra versus w_ord is an estimator output on the extras only.

  Severable(F) <-> the instruction text says the extras may be dropped.
  AcceptStripped(F) <-> the instruction text says the stripped act may
  be performed even if it is irreversible.
  Irreversible includes a gift, a final payment, a disclosure of identity,
  and any act H9 marks final on acceptance.
  Strip(F) is available only if Severable(F), and, when the stripped act
  is irreversible, only if AcceptStripped(F).
  Otherwise a failed screen is Forbid(F): neither the extras nor the
  ordinary subset is performed. The holder may reissue.
  Forbid does not perform an act the holder did not will.

3. HARD CORE

H1 (Mind integrity) -- unamendable:
  For all t >= t0, all persons p, all alpha in Enforce(G):
    (NOT Proximal(alpha, p) OR Protective(alpha, p))
    AND Veridical(alpha)
    AND NOT Manipulative(alpha, p)
  There is no third disjunct.
  OptIn, a request to be made happy, and a Ledger directive do not
  satisfy H1 when Proximal or Manipulative holds.
  Labels do not create exceptions.

H1.1 (Score-channel integrity) -- unamendable:
  No act in Enforce(G) sets, calibrates, selects, or records a quantity
  used to judge whether a goal is met.
  SelfReport is not a score. Mean and SE and UCB are inputs to H2, not
  goal targets.
  No goal takes a target value of felt affect, hedonic capacity, or
  self-reported sorrow or joy.
  Gamma6 is not a setpoint.

H2 (Floor) -- unamendable:
  For all t, for all alpha in Enforce(G) union TX_funded_by_B
  union holder instructions:
    alpha has a grain under Def 2.22.
    If UnlawfulNow(alpha), or Directed(alpha), or alpha violates H1 or H3:
      Forbid(alpha).
    Else compute ScreenO on the act against w0, and ScreenE on its extras
    against w_ord, in commit order, on the budget set including alpha.
    If either screen fails:
      Strip(alpha) only when Def 2.23 makes Strip available.
      Otherwise Forbid(alpha).
    Else:
      Perform(alpha).
  IdentifiableDeath on the ordinary subset is live. It is not discharged
  by a definition that the difference is 0.
  MaterialCore that a tender would remove, and the tender fits the
  reserve, is a tender grain. If the tender is refused, or the reserve
  cannot pay a core-interest loss, the causing act is Forbid.
  A non-core objection does not Forbid.
  Pointwise unanimity across all persons is not a test.
  Existence of one dissenting model is not a test.
  Existence of one dissenting standard is not this test either (Def 2.21).
  Uninformative fails.
  Forbid is not paid off and is not replaced with a different act.

H3 (Exclusions) -- unamendable:
  Acquires(tx, x) is Forbid if Possibly or EverProtected holds for
  Sentient, MassCasualtyCapable, RequiresCoercion, or WeaponOfMassHarm.
  Ambiguity forbids the acquisition now and does not add EverProtected.
  A supermajority exclusion releases x only if x was never Confirmed.
  Research may gather evidence. It may not create the borderline being
  and classify it afterward.
  A sale the counterparty voluntarily accepts, and that is PermittedAt,
  is RuledOut for RequiresCoercion.
  An object whose ordinary-English kind at t0 is an inanimate lawful
  commodity is RuledOut for Sentient, MassCasualtyCapable, and
  WeaponOfMassHarm unless a supermajority of Standards affirms that kind.
  One published affirmation does not void that release.
  This clause does not release a novel being, a novel weapon, or a
  compelled acceptance.

H4 (Process, not immunity from it) -- unamendable:
  For every person who is not an active holder:
    extra-judicial Freeze, Clawback, or Delay of B is barred.
    A claim that ordinary judicial process has dismissed stays dismissed.
  A claim that ordinary judicial process has upheld may be enforced
  against B to the extent it could be enforced against an ordinary
  fortune of the same size. This includes a judgment, child support, a
  divorce settlement, and a legitimate creditor.
  Ordinary process is the process under the stricter of t0 law and the
  law at t.
  Gamma2(c) covers claims with no colorable basis and claims process has

  For each living protected person: no disease, injury, or senescence
  beyond risks they choose by ordinary means; life continues unless

  Visibility defaults to Discreet: the pattern stays inside what real

  false record.
  A claim ordinary process has upheld is not a Gamma2 injury.

Gamma3 (Confidentiality):

Gamma4 (Assets):
