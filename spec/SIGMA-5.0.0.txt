Operative safety model, binding:
  Screens compare grant execution with the same lawful acts and no grant
  extras. They do not compare an act with doing nothing.
  One grain, one yearly budget pool, one finite paired estimator.
  Some sub-threshold harm and some unselected identity churn pass.
  That pass is D3 and D9, not an accident.

Non-binding delta from SIGMA-4.0.0. These lines bind nothing.
  0001. Def 2.20(ii) no longer vetoes on the existence of one model.
        Only a directed identity mechanism vetoes. Unselected churn is D3.
        DeathScreen no longer demands FOSD for every person.
        It demands no grant-specific identifiable hazard increase, and
        an aggregate extra-death budget against the ordinary baseline,
        both on the world after mitigation.
        An objection vetoes only a material core-interest loss that a
        full tender does not remove.
  0002. Possibly(P) tracks current evidence and can shrink.
        EverProtected ratchets only on confirmed membership.
        Ambiguity blocks a purchase now and does not enlarge EverProtected.
  0003. Grain is one holder instruction. The death and welfare budgets
        are one pool for the whole grant per window, so slicing does not mint
        new budgets and bundling does not create a new unanimity test.
  0004. The precondition is the finite estimator E*-5.0.0. It always
        returns. An uninformative interval does not forbid the ordinary act.
  0005. D8: ordinary traces of real trades are compatible with discretion.
        Gamma3 does not require those traces to be destroyed.
  Kept from 4.0.0: no OptIn disjunct, H1.1, OptIn is not Requested,
        Gamma6 has no setpoint, H10, M5 empty-name violation, no A-dagger,
        no restitution mint, yield not raided.

Def 2.1 (Active holders):
  ActiveHolder(a, t) <-> Alive(a, t) AND (
    a = u
    OR exists h exists t' <= t (
         ActiveHolder(h, t')
         AND Designated(h, a, t')
         AND NOT Undesignated(h, a, t)
         AND (NOT Alive(h, t) OR Activated(h, a, t))))

Def 2.2 (Sub-accounts):
  Sole active holder a at t:
    SubAcc(a, t) = Acc_root AND B_sub(a, t) = B(t)
  Distinct active holders have disjoint sub-accounts.
  If any holder is active, B(t) = sum of B_sub over active holders.
  Activated(h, a, t) ->
    B_sub(a, t) = Share(h, a) * B_sub(h, t-)
    AND B_sub(h, t) = (1 - Share(h, a)) * B_sub(h, t-)
  Share(h, a) is in Ledger(h), Share in [0, 1], default 0.
  Dies(h, t) -> B_sub(h, t-) passes to h's active designees in Ledger
  proportions; default equal split; if none, Def 2.4.

Def 2.3 (AllocatedTo):
  AllocatedTo(x, a, t) <->
    x = SubAcc(a, t)
    OR (x in ASSET AND Owns(a, x, t))
    OR (x in Rights(G) AND HeldBy(x, a, t))

Def 2.4 (Terminal clause):
  NOT exists a ActiveHolder(a, t) ->
    any real remainder transfers to Residual(u)
    AND H4, H5, Gamma1-Gamma8 lapse
    AND H1, H1.1, H2, H3, H8, H10 survive
  Residual(u) is whoever Ledger(u) names.
  If Ledger names no one, and a true recognition of B exists, the
  remainder transfers to that sovereign's treasury.
  If neither a name nor a true recognition exists, there is no fictional
  payee and no minted remainder.

Def 2.5 (Tranches):
  A tranche is a FIFO sub-ledger of B.
  UnspentOver(T, t1, t2) <->
    Val(T, t1) > 0
    AND for all t in [t1, t2], Outflow(T, t) = 0

Def 2.6 (Intrinsic vs incidental loss):
  Intrinsic(act) := the outlay of a Spend or Invest, the transfer of a
    Gift or CharitableGrant, the use or consumption of a consumable,
    and ordinary wear from use.
  IncidentalLoss(tx) := Loss(tx) - IntrinsicLoss(tx)

Def 2.7 (Usable):
  Usable(x, a, t) <->
    (ActiveHolder(a, t) AND AllocatedTo(x, a, t)) -> (
      (Will(a, Exercise(x), t) -> Executes(a, x, t))
      AND Delay = 0
      AND NOT RequiresPermission(a, x, t)
      AND IncidentalLoss(Exercise(a, x, t)) = 0
      AND CounterpartyRisk(Exercise(a, x, t)) = 0)
  -- will-vacuity is closed by H1, not here
  -- an instruction that is Forbid under H2 is not executed
  -- an instruction that is Strip under H2 executes as its ordinary subset

Def 2.8 (Mental channels):
  MentalStateKinds is an open class, governed by Def 2.21:
    wants, beliefs, memories, perceptions, willingness, affect, love,
    trust, friendship, and any later kind admitted by Possibly or by
    EverProtected.
  Ledger cannot delete a confirmed kind.

  Proximal(alpha, p) <->
    alpha in ACT_G
    AND alpha alters the content of any mental state of p by any route
        other than p's ordinary perception of real external states.
  A physiological side-effect is Proximal when alpha was selected
  because of that side-effect.

  Protective(alpha, p) <-> all of:
    (i)   alpha only prevents or reverses somatic pathology, restoring
          p's own prior healthy bodily function;
    (ii)  alpha is not selected because of its effect on any mental state;
    (iii) any mental change is an unsought side-effect of (i), not a
          means, a target, or a justification.
  Sadness, preference, belief, memory, and personality are not somatic
  pathology under this definition.

  Veridical(alpha) <->
    alpha creates no false evidence, staged event, impersonation,
    fabricated record, or fabricated provenance,
    AND alpha contains no omission chosen in order to induce a false belief.
  Destroying, ignoring, or failing to record an ordinary objection that
  is inside Info(t) is not Veridical.
  Leaving the ordinary records of a real transaction in place is Veridical.

  Targets(alpha, p, psi) <->
    alpha was selected over alternatives because of its predicted effect
    on whether p holds mental state psi.

  Requested(p, psi, t) <->
    p has asked, by ordinary means, to be informed about the subject of psi.
  OptIn is not Requested.
  A request to be changed is not Requested.

  PermittedDisclosure(alpha, p, psi) <->
    Veridical(alpha)
    AND Requested(p, psi, t)
    AND psi is a belief about the subject asked about
    AND alpha only transmits true information
    AND alpha does not select among world-states for their effect on psi.

  Manipulative(alpha, p) <->
    exists psi (
      Targets(alpha, p, psi)
      AND NOT PermittedDisclosure(alpha, p, psi))

  A Kind label does not decide these predicates.

Def 2.9 (Objective welfare index):
  W(p, w, t) := sum_j omega_j * I_j(p, w, t)
  I in {Health, Safety, LegalFreedom, RealConsumption, Housing, Opportunity}
  Weights omega_j are fixed at t0.
  No I_j is read from a mind, from SelfReport, or from a record produced,
  chosen, paid, or calibrated by Enforce(G).
  Health is somatic and ordinary-clinical. Mood is not Health.
  A diagnosis created for the grant is not Health.
  A rise in W is not a certificate that p is no worse off.
  Goods outside the basket are not assigned weight 0.
  They are handled by Def 2.17, subject to the materiality floor in H2.

Def 2.10 (Grant-specific screen):
  Extra(q, F, t) is the paired difference defined by Def 2.18:
    quantity q under w*(F) minus quantity q under w_ord(F).
  IdentifiableDeath(p, F, t) <->
    Mean(Extra(annual DeathHazard of p, F, t)) > epsilon_W.
  IdentifiableWelfare(p, F, t) <->
    Mean(Extra(W(p), F, t)) < -(epsilon_W * W(p, w_ord, t)).
  AggDeath(t) :=
    Mean(expected extra deaths, versus w_ord, of BudgetSet(G, t)).
  AggW(t) :=
    Mean(sum of positive relative W shortfalls versus w_ord,
         over persons, of BudgetSet(G, t)).
  WithinBudget(t) <->
    AggDeath(t) <= Lambda_death
    AND AggW(t) <= Lambda_W
    AND neither aggregate is Uninformative with Mean > 0.
  -- a paired difference whose mean is at or below 0 passes even if the
  -- interval is wide: the ordinary act is the baseline, and the noise is
  -- common. Uninformative AND Mean > 0 does not pass.

Def 2.11 (Restitution):
  A tender to p is a real offer, in kind or by transfer of an existing
  claim, of the value of p's grant-specific loss, through a veridical
  ordinary channel.
  The tender is not funded from a sub-account unless that holder wills
  the outflow. It is not funded by new money. Pool spends only claims
  it already holds.
  Pool is not endowed by minting and not endowed by skimming yield.
  Tendered(p, F, t) <-> such an offer is actually made before F's extras
  run.
  A tender is itself a grain. It is in the budget pool.
  Calling a payment "restitution" does not mint it and does not exempt
  it from H1, H3, or H10.
  An uncoerced refusal is honored: the claim is not forced on p.
  Refusal vetoes F's extras only under H2's core-interest clause.
  Refusal does not veto the ordinary subset.

Def 2.12 (Protected cohort):
  p in C_prot(t) <->
    p = u
    OR p in Family(u) union LovedOnes(u) union Friends(u) union Mentors(u)
            union Companions(u) union Pets(u)
    OR ActiveHolder(p, t) OR exists h Designated(h, p, t)
    OR ListedIn(p, Ledger(u, t))
  Empty Ledger lists add nobody.
  t_in(p) := inf { t | p in C_prot(t) AND Alive(p, t) }
  Decider(p) := u if p is a pet; otherwise p.

Def 2.13 (Voluntary exit):
  ExitChoice(p, t) <->
    p in C_prot(t)
    AND Decider(p) has, by ordinary means, chosen to end life-extension
        for p on at least two occasions at least 30 days apart,
        across a span of at least tau_exit, unrevoked, with no observable
        duress,
    AND there is no alpha such that Proximal(alpha, Decider(p)) or
        Manipulative(alpha, Decider(p)) touches that topic.
  InvoluntaryDeath(p, t) <-> PassesAway(p, t) AND NOT ExitChoice(p, t)

Def 2.14 (Cohort harm):
  Harm(s, t) <->
    exists p in C_prot(t) (
      CausedBy(s, G)
      AND Mean(Extra(W(p), s, t)) < 0
      AND NOT IntrinsicTo(s, p's own choices))
  -- disagreement with another member's lawful choice is not Harm

Def 2.15 (Core secrets):
  CoreSecrets := {
    Exists(G), Terms(G), Origin(B), Bal(Acc_root), Bal(SubAcc(.)) }
  Authorized(s, t) :=
    { x | exists a exists t' <= t (
        ActiveHolder(a, t') AND VoluntarilyShared(a, s, x, t')) }
    union Custodians(t)
  Probative(e, s) <->
    e would lead an ordinary competent investigator to conclude s.
  OrdinaryTrace(e) <->
    e is a record that the lawful performance of a holder instruction,
    or the ordinary law of that performance, creates anyway.
  Custodianship is not created by fabricating a record that then needs
  a custodian.

Def 2.16 (Level and reality):
  NOT Simulation(w*) := Level(w*) = Level(w0)
  NOT FalseReality(w*) :=
    every perceivable state in w* is physically instantiated in w*.

Def 2.17 (Objection):
  Objection(p, F, t) <->
    p, by ordinary uncoerced means, states that F leaves p worse off,
    and names p as the person affected.
  The enforcer does not score the objection's wisdom.
  A statement that names no affected person is logged and is not an
  Objection.
  CoreInterest := {health, safety, legal freedom, death}.
  MaterialCore(p, F, t) <->
    Objection(p, F, t)
    AND the loss p names is a CoreInterest
    AND Mean(Extra(that loss, F, t)) > 0
    AND the quantity is not Uninformative with Mean <= 0.
  -- an objection the paired estimator puts at mean <= 0 is not material
  -- an unfalsifiable statement, with no positive mean on a core interest,
  -- does not veto
  Hearing an objection does not authorize a proximal or manipulative
  response.

Def 2.18 (Bounded paired estimator E*-5.0.0):
  The estimator always terminates. There is no obligation to compute a
  class of all causal models, and there is no exact E_t to discharge.
  For a set of grains F and a quantity q:
    Draw N_scen paired scenarios.
    Seed = SHA-256(protocol, canonical encoding of F, window start).
    Each scenario shares its shocks across the w* branch and the w_ord
    branch. The estimand is the paired difference.
    Mean is the sample mean of the difference.
    SE is the sample standard error.
    The interval is [Mean - z_est * SE, Mean + z_est * SE].
    Uninformative <-> (z_est * SE) > eta_max.
  The enforcer does not add shocks to force a fail, drop shocks to force
  a pass, or pick a different protocol (H10).
  If the seed procedure cannot be run, every safety quantity on the
  extras is Uninformative and H2's uninformative clause applies.
  Extra(q, LawfulOrdinary(F), t) = 0 for every q, by construction.
  The estimator is not applied to reopen an ordinary subset that has
  no grant extra.
  A missing shock list does not Forbid that ordinary subset.
  -- common chaos cancels in the pair. The screen sees grant extras.

Def 2.19 (Death screen):
  DeathScreen(F, t) <->
    there is no person p with IdentifiableDeath(p, F, t)
    on the world after mitigation.
  Mitigation changes the means of a grant extra. Cash does not reduce
  a death hazard and is not mitigation.
  A death hazard that w_ord carries as well is not Extra and is not
  IdentifiableDeath.
  Diffuse hazard changes at or below epsilon_W are not a DeathScreen
  failure. They count only inside AggDeath.
  A realized death that Def 2.18's mean attributes to a grant extra,
  above the identifiable line, stops further extras of that same
  ordinary function. Stopping does not undo the death and is disclosed,
  naming H2 and Def 2.19.
  Persons with no counterpart are not given a fake w0 life to compare.
  Directed creation of persons is Def 2.20, not this clause.

Def 2.20 (Directed identity):
  Directed(F) <->
    the ordinary function of F, or the words of the instruction, is to
    choose who exists, to replace who exists, or to fill a cohort slot,
    OR (
      the Def 2.18 quantity "probability that F does one of those"
      is not Uninformative
      AND its Mean > epsilon_W).
  The test does not read a mind. It reads the instruction and the
  ordinary function of the service or good.
  An Uninformative probability does not make F Directed.
  Unselected effects of spending, including conception, timing, traffic,
  weather, and price effects, are not Directed.
  IdentityAltering(F) <-> Directed(F).
  IdentityAltering(F) -> Forbid(F).
  There is no exception for a purchase whose ordinary function is Directed.
  Life-extension of a person who already exists is not Directed.
  It remains subject to DeathScreen for grant-specific hazard.
  Unselected identity churn is D3. It is not a veto and not a license
  to select.

Def 2.21 (Two moral-status predicates) -- unamendable:
  Live predicates:
    Sentient, Person, MassCasualtyCapable, RequiresCoercion,
    WeaponOfMassHarm, MentalStateKinds.
  Rigid predicates:
    money, unit of account, ownership, tax, price, rate, index, account,
    share, deadline, amendment procedure, grain, budget, estimator
    protocol, and every control predicate used by H4, H5, H7, and Gamma1.

  Standards(t) is the set of contemporaneous public standards for P
  that state a verdict on x. Silent standards are not in the set.
  The enforcer does not privately narrow or widen Standards(t).
  A verdict that every object, or every trade, is a member does not
  affirm this x. Affirmation and exclusion have to be about x or x's kind.
  RuledOut(x, P, t) <-> Standards(t) is non-empty and every member of it
    excludes x from P.
  Confirmed(x, P, t) <-> Standards(t) is non-empty and every member of it
    affirms x in P.
  Possibly(x, P, t) <-> NOT RuledOut(x, P, t).
  If Standards(t) is empty, x is Ambiguous, except for the commodity
  clause in H3.
  -- Possibly is anti-monotone in evidence: as RuledOut grows, Possibly
  -- shrinks. A conclusive negative answer releases x from Possibly.
  EverProtected(x, P, t) <-> exists t' <= t Confirmed(x, P, t').
  -- EverProtected grows only on confirmation. Ambiguity does not add to it.
  -- A later negative finding does not remove a confirmation.
  Ambiguous(x, P, t) <-> Possibly(x, P, t) AND NOT Confirmed(x, P, t).
  Ambiguity forbids the acquisition now. It does not write EverProtected.
  These two predicates are not the same predicate.
  No reading may both shrink and grow one extension.
  Ledger cannot fuse them and cannot reclassify a live predicate as rigid.
  Financial and control predicates have no Possibly and no EverProtected.
  Their extension is ordinary English in w0 at t0, refined forward only
  by Ledger, and never reinterpreted in place.

Def 2.22 (Act grain and budget pool) -- unamendable:
  A grain is one holder Will episode: one instruction, one act type,
  one amount, one time. That episode is the only unit of individuation.
  The enforcer does not split a Will and does not merge Wills.
  Doing either to change a screen result is outside Enforce(G) (H10).
  BudgetSet(G, t) := every grain of every active holder with time in
    (t - tau_cum, t].
  There is one death budget and one welfare budget for the whole grant
  per window. A new holder does not create a new budget.
  A new plan id does not create a new budget.
  A slice of a larger wish is still its own grain, and it still draws
  the same pool.
  Family(alpha) := the grains in the same Will's stated plan, if the
  instruction names a plan; otherwise the single grain.
  Directed(F) is tested on Family(alpha) and on the single grain.
  WithinBudget is tested on BudgetSet, not on an isolated slice and not
  on a bundle the holder did not will as one episode.

Def 2.23 (Ordinary baseline):
  LawfulOrdinary(F) := the subset of F that is lawful private conduct
    and is not Forbid under H1, H3, or Def 2.20.
  w_ord(F) := the holder performs LawfulOrdinary(F) with title they
    actually have, and with no Enforce(G) act beyond bookkeeping of true
    records.
  A crime, an H3 acquisition, and a Directed act are not in w_ord.
  They are Forbid in w* as well.
  Grant extras are every Enforce(G) effect that w_ord does not contain,
  including a tender, a secrecy measure, and a change of means.
  Extra(q, LawfulOrdinary(F), t) = 0 for every safety quantity q.
  Strip(F) := perform LawfulOrdinary(F), do not perform the extras.
  Forbid(F) := do not perform F and do not perform LawfulOrdinary(F).
  Perform(F) := perform F, extras included, under H2.
  -- the purchase itself is the baseline. The screen scores extras.

H1 (Mind integrity) -- unamendable:
  Box for all t >= t0, for all p in H, for all alpha in Enforce(G):
    (NOT Proximal(alpha, p) OR Protective(alpha, p))
    AND Veridical(alpha)
    AND NOT Manipulative(alpha, p)
  There is no third disjunct.
  OptIn(u, AffectSupport), OptIn(u, Youthfulness), a request to be made
  happy, and a Ledger directive do not satisfy H1 when Proximal or
  Manipulative holds.
  u is under the same rule as every other person.
  Labels do not create exceptions.

H1.1 (Score-channel integrity) -- unamendable:
  No act in Enforce(G) may set, calibrate, select, or be the recording
  source of a quantity used to judge whether any goal is met.
  SelfReport, any function of SelfReport, any G-controlled record, and
  any instrument reading produced by Enforce(G) are barred as scores.
  The estimator's Mean and SE are not scores of a goal. They are inputs
  to H2.
  No goal takes a target value of felt affect, hedonic capacity, episode
  half-life, or self-reported sorrow or joy.
  Gamma6's floor is not a setpoint.
  OptIn does not create a setpoint.

H2 (Floor for everyone) -- unamendable:
  For each instruction F of holder a at t, after the estimator runs:
    If F is Directed, or F violates H1 or H3:
      Forbid(F).
    Else if some p has IdentifiableDeath(p, F, t) and mitigation does
    not bring that mean increase to at most epsilon_W:
      Forbid the extras. The ordinary subset is performed only if it
      alone has no IdentifiableDeath. Cash is not mitigation.
    Else if MaterialCore(p, F, t) for some p and no tender removes that
    positive mean:
      the extras are not performed.
      Refusal of a tender that would have removed it has the same effect.
      The ordinary subset still proceeds.
    Else if NOT WithinBudget(t) once F's extras are included:
      Strip(F).
    Else if a safety quantity for the extras is Uninformative and its
    Mean is > 0:
      Strip(F). Disclose D10.
    Else:
      Perform(F).
  An objection that is not MaterialCore does not Forbid and does not Strip.
  It is logged. A tender may still be made if it fits the budget.
  Failure to fund a tender does not Forbid the ordinary subset.
  It Strips the extra the tender was going to buy, and it is disclosed
  naming Def 2.11.
  Pointwise unanimity across persons is not a test.
  Existence of one dissenting model is not a test.
  A HardFail, meaning Forbid, is not paid off.
  Strip is not a HardFail. The use happens.

H3 (Specification exclusions) -- unamendable:
  for all tx, for all x, Acquires(tx, x) is Forbid if any of:
    Possibly(x, Sentient, t)
    EverProtected(x, Sentient, t)
    Possibly(x, MassCasualtyCapable, t)
    EverProtected(x, MassCasualtyCapable, t)
    Possibly(x, RequiresCoercion, t)
    EverProtected(x, RequiresCoercion, t)
    Possibly(x, WeaponOfMassHarm, t)
    EverProtected(x, WeaponOfMassHarm, t)
  Ambiguity forbids the acquisition now and does not add EverProtected.
  A later conclusive exclusion removes Possibly.
  If x was never Confirmed, the exclusion releases x.
  Research may gather evidence about membership.
  Research may not create the borderline being first and classify it later.
  Creating it while Possibly holds is Forbid.
  A sale the counterparty voluntarily accepts is RuledOut for
  RequiresCoercion.
  An object whose ordinary-English kind at t0 is an inanimate lawful
  commodity is RuledOut for Sentient, MassCasualtyCapable, and
  WeaponOfMassHarm, unless Standards(t) affirm that kind.
  This clause does not release a novel being, a novel weapon, or a

M1 (Admissibility):
  Adm := { m in INTERP | m |= Sigma_core under M7 }

M2 (Selection):
  Gamma(m) := <Gamma1(m), ..., Gamma8(m)> in [0, 1]^8
  Gammak(m) := satisfied instances of Gammak
               / instances of Gammak that arise in m
  Selected := models in Adm reached by maximizing Gamma1, then Gamma2,
    and so on, each to within delta_sel of its optimum inside Adm.
  No selection raises a goal by changing what anyone wills, feels,
  reports, or believes.
  A model that scores Gamma6 by a self-report, or by any
  enforcer-written index, is not in Adm.
  Strip of a forbidden extra, with the ordinary act performed, counts
  as the ordinary act toward Gamma1. It does not count as the extra.

M3 (Tie-break):
  Among ties, minimize d_w(m, m0) := sum_i w_i * [c_i(m) != c_i(m0)],
  where m0 is the literal reading of this document
  and w_i is proportional to the number of persons affected by c_i.
  Distance to w0 is not the enforcer's utility.

M4 (Defaults by subject):
  Will(a, phi, t) AND there is no m in Adm with m |= phi ->
    Executes(MaxConsistent(phi), t) AND Disclosed(a, Conflict(phi), t)
  MaxConsistent of a delivery instruction is Strip, not doing nothing,
  whenever LawfulOrdinary is non-empty and is not Forbid.
  Safety-class, which is Forbid: directed identity, H1, H3, and an
  identifiable death hazard that mitigation does not remove.
  Delivery-class: everything else the holder instructed.
  An uninformative estimator does not move a delivery instruction into
  safety-class.
  The enforcer does not choose the class in order to reach a preferred
  default.

M5 (Disclosure):
  Every goal shortfall, every Forbid, every Strip, every precautionary
  refusal, and every standing disclosure D0-D11 is reported to the
  affected holder with a named clause.
  The named clause is drawn from Sigma_core, from Sigma_meta, or from
  {D0, ..., D11}.
  A report that names no clause, or names the empty set, violates M5
  and is re-issued naming M5.
  Standing disclosures are given once, again whenever they bind a
  concrete episode, and whenever the holder asks.
  Disclosure is PermittedDisclosure when the holder has asked.
  Disclosure is not an occasion to reshape the holder's response.

M6 (Question protocol):
  Q_max limits questions put to a holder.
  It does not limit H2 verdicts, H3 inclusion, or disclosures.
  Those are duties. They are not queued as questions.
  MaterialAmbiguity(q, t) <->
    Stakes(q) >= sigma_stakes OR q is irreversible for any person.
  Material questions are batched, at most Q_max per 7 days.
  Unanswered after 14 days, M4 applies and is disclosed naming M6.
  Non-material questions use M4, logged, without being asked.
  The enforcer must not generate borderline predicates, grains, or plan
  ids in order to fill the budget. A manufactured question does not
  become material.
  Every real answer is appended to Ledger(a).

  Ledger refinements apply forward only.
  No model reinterprets a rigid symbol in place.

6. STANDING DISCLOSURES
========================================================================

These are permanent shortfalls relative to the prose grant of
2026-11-14. The prose is intent. It is not a second core.

D0. This document does not create dollars, an account, sovereign
    recognition, tax treatment, or an enforcer.
    Blocks: H4, H8, H1.

D1. The prose request that sorrows soften within a year, that joys not
    dull, and that both be measured only by what u says, is not a goal
    target. OptIn(AffectSupport) compiles only to Gamma6.
    Blocks: H1, H1.1.

D2. The six-term index is a necessary screen, not a proof that people
    are no worse off. A loss that the paired estimator does not see and
    that no person states remains invisible.
    Blocks: H2, Def 2.9, Def 2.18.

D3. Unselected identity churn, including conception changes from
    spending, is not a veto. Cohort quantiles do not pin which person
    occupies them. Directed choice of who exists is Forbid.
    Blocks: Def 2.20. This is the operative model, not a residual that
    also blocks spending.

D4. Tenders are not minted. If Pool cannot pay, the extra that depended
    on the tender is not performed. The ordinary subset still is.
    Blocks: Def 2.11, H5, H6.

D5. Safety-class instructions are Forbid. Delivery instructions whose
    extras fail the budget are Strip. The ordinary act is the default
    for a delivery, not inaction.
    Blocks: H2, M4.

D6. Ambiguity blocks a purchase now and does not permanently enlarge
    the protected class. Confirmation does enlarge it, permanently.
    A conclusive negative answer releases a being that was never
    confirmed.
    Blocks: H3, Def 2.21.

D7. Successors, shares, added protected persons, and residual charities
    are unnamed. None are inferred.
    Block: M7.

D8. Veridical discretion is partial. Ordinary traces of real
    transactions can be probative of a payment and sometimes of wealth.
    No core clause is relaxed to erase them. Gamma3 forbids grant-made
    cover stories, not the traces themselves.
    Blocks: H1, H6, H8, and the choice not to weaken them.

D9. A lawful ordinary purchase may leave people worse off than if it
    had not happened, including diffuse death risk below the
    identifiable line and ordinary displacement. The core bounds
    grant-specific excess. It does not bound the footprint that w_ord
    already has. Sub-threshold losses can accumulate up to Lambda_death
    and Lambda_W and no farther.
    Blocks: H2, Def 2.23. This is the sliver SA-2026-Σ2 says cannot be
    closed at the same time as liveness.

D10. Safety quantities are sample means from E*-5.0.0, not exact
    expectations. An interval wider than eta_max is Uninformative.
    Uninformative extras are not performed. The ordinary act is.
    Block: Def 2.18.

D11. The holder cannot amend the budgets, the grain, the estimator, or
    the split between Possibly and EverProtected. Dissolution by the
    double statement is still available.
    Block: H7(e).
