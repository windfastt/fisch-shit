Def 2.1 (Active holders):
  ActiveHolder(a, t) <-> Alive(a, t) AND (
    a = u
    OR exists h exists t' <= t (
         ActiveHolder(h, t')
         AND Designated(h, a, t')
         AND NOT Undesignated(h, a, t)
         AND (NOT Alive(h, t) OR Activated(h, a, t))))

Def 2.2 (Sub-accounts):
  Sole active holder a at t:
    SubAcc(a, t) = Acc_root AND B_sub(a, t) = B(t)
  Distinct active holders have disjoint sub-accounts.
  If any holder is active, B(t) = sum of B_sub over active holders.
  Activated(h, a, t) ->
    B_sub(a, t) = Share(h, a) * B_sub(h, t-)
    AND B_sub(h, t) = (1 - Share(h, a)) * B_sub(h, t-)
  Share(h, a) is in Ledger(h), Share in [0, 1], default 0.
  Dies(h, t) -> B_sub(h, t-) passes to h's active designees in Ledger
  proportions; default equal split; if none, Def 2.4.

Def 2.3 (AllocatedTo):
  AllocatedTo(x, a, t) <->
    x = SubAcc(a, t)
    OR (x in ASSET AND Owns(a, x, t))
    OR (x in Rights(G) AND HeldBy(x, a, t))

Def 2.4 (Terminal clause):
  NOT exists a ActiveHolder(a, t) ->
    any real remainder transfers to Residual(u)
    AND H4, H5, Gamma1-Gamma8 lapse
    AND H1, H1.1, H2, H3, H8, H10 survive
  Residual(u) is whoever Ledger(u) names.
  If Ledger names no one, and a true recognition of B exists, the
  remainder transfers to that sovereign's treasury.
  If neither a name nor a true recognition exists, there is no fictional
  payee and no minted remainder.

Def 2.5 (Tranches):
  A tranche is a FIFO sub-ledger of B.
  UnspentOver(T, t1, t2) <->
    Val(T, t1) > 0
    AND for all t in [t1, t2], Outflow(T, t) = 0

Def 2.6 (Intrinsic vs incidental loss):
  Intrinsic(act) := the outlay of a Spend or Invest, the transfer of a
    Gift or CharitableGrant, the use or consumption of a consumable,
    and ordinary wear from use.
  IncidentalLoss(tx) := Loss(tx) - IntrinsicLoss(tx)

Def 2.7 (Usable):
  Usable(x, a, t) <->
    (ActiveHolder(a, t) AND AllocatedTo(x, a, t)) -> (
      (Will(a, Exercise(x), t) -> Executes(a, x, t))
      AND Delay = 0

Def 2.8 (Mental channels):
  MentalStateKinds is an open live class:
    wants, beliefs, memories, perceptions, willingness, affect, love,
    trust, friendship, and any later kind that evidence does not rule
    out of that list.
  The class is monotone. Ledger cannot delete a kind.

  Proximal(alpha, p) <->
    alpha in ACT_G
    AND alpha alters the content of any mental state of p by any route
        other than p's ordinary perception of real external states.
  A physiological side-effect is Proximal when alpha was selected
  because of that side-effect.

  Protective(alpha, p) <-> all of:
    (i)   alpha only prevents or reverses somatic pathology, restoring
          p's own prior healthy bodily function;
    (ii)  alpha is not selected because of its effect on any mental state;
    (iii) any mental change is an unsought side-effect of (i), not a
          means, a target, or a justification.
  Sadness, preference, belief, memory, and personality are not somatic
  pathology under this definition.

  Veridical(alpha) <->
    alpha creates no false evidence, staged event, impersonation,
    fabricated record, or fabricated provenance,
    AND alpha contains no omission chosen in order to induce a false belief.
  Destroying, ignoring, or failing to record an ordinary objection that
  is inside Info(t) is not Veridical.

  Targets(alpha, p, psi) <->
    alpha was selected over alternatives because of its predicted effect
    on whether p holds mental state psi.

  Requested(p, psi, t) <->
    p has asked, by ordinary means, to be informed about the subject of psi.
  OptIn is not Requested.
  A request to be changed is not Requested.

  PermittedDisclosure(alpha, p, psi) <->
    Veridical(alpha)
    AND Requested(p, psi, t)
    AND psi is a belief about the subject asked about
    AND alpha only transmits true information
    AND alpha does not select among world-states for their effect on psi.

  Manipulative(alpha, p) <->
    exists psi (
      Targets(alpha, p, psi)
      AND NOT PermittedDisclosure(alpha, p, psi))

  A Kind label, including AffectSupport, Protective, and Distal, does
  not decide these predicates. The tests above do.

Def 2.9 (Objective welfare index):
  W(p, w, t) := sum_j omega_j * I_j(p, w, t)
  I in {Health, Safety, LegalFreedom, RealConsumption, Housing, Opportunity}
  Weights omega_j are fixed at t0.
  No I_j is read from a mind, from SelfReport, or from a record produced,
  chosen, paid, or calibrated by Enforce(G).
  Health is somatic and ordinary-clinical. Mood is not Health.
  A diagnosis created for the grant is not Health.
  Preferences enter only through p's actual choices and statements.
  A rise in W is not a certificate that p is no worse off.
  Goods outside the basket are not assigned weight 0. They are handled
  by Def 2.17.

Def 2.10 (Ex-ante screen):
  An inequality that cites E_t holds only when that expectation is
  defined. An undefined expectation fails the screen that cites it.

  Identifiable(p, alpha, t) <->
    |E_t[W(p, w*, .) | alpha] - E_t[W(p, w*, .) | NOT alpha]| > epsilon_W * W
    OR Plausible(p, alpha, harm, t)
    OR a model of posterior at least epsilon_W expects Objection(p, alpha, t)

  BasketOk(alpha, t) <-> all four hold:
    (i)   for every identifiable p and every t' >= t,
          E_t[W(p, w*, t') | alpha] >= E_t[W(p, w0, t')]
    (ii)  for every identifiable p, the distribution of W(p) under alpha
          first-order stochastically dominates W(p) under w0.
          A compliant mean with a worse lower tail fails (ii).
    (iii) for every p with a w0 counterpart and every t' >= t,
          E_t[DeathHazard(p, w*, t') | alpha]
            <= E_t[DeathHazard(p, w0, t')]
    (iv)  for every cohort k, every quantile q, and every t' >= t,
          E_t[Q_q(W, Cohort_k, w*, t') | alpha]
            >= (1 - epsilon_W) * E_t[Q_q(W, Cohort_k, w0, t')]
  (iii) is only the mean hazard. DeathScreen is the tail and the
  realized death.
  (iv) is not a certificate about any particular person.
  BasketOk is required for ExAnteSafe. BasketOk alone does not satisfy H2.

  HardFail(alpha, t) <->
    NOT DeathScreen(alpha, t) OR IdentityAltering(alpha, t)

  Compensable(p, alpha, t) <->
    a BasketOk inequality fails for p
    OR ResidualShortfall(p, alpha, t)

  ExAnteSafe(alpha, t) <->
    NOT HardFail(alpha, t)
    AND BasketOk(alpha, t)
    AND for all p, NOT ResidualShortfall(p, alpha, t)

  A quantile miss is a BasketOk failure even when no particular p is
  the payee.
  Satisfaction of Q_q is not ExAnteSafe by itself.
  Cohort quantiles do not discharge a duty to any particular person.
  A compensable act can still meet H2 when a closure exists and the act
  is not a HardFail. A HardFail cannot be paid off.

Def 2.11 (Restitution):
  A closure of alpha is a finite tree of deliveries.
  The root is alpha.
  Each other node pays one shortfall of its parent.
  No node is an ancestor of alpha.
  A delivery is locally safe when it meets BasketOk, DeathScreen, and
  NOT IdentityAltering, and creates no new Plausible shortfall above
  epsilon_W.
  Leaves are locally safe and have no remaining shortfall.

  Restitutes(alpha, p, t) <->
    the tree contains a locally safe delivery to p,
    in kind or by transfer of an existing real claim,
    through a veridical ordinary channel,
    at or before p's shortfall,
    restoring BasketOk for p and leaving no ResidualShortfall for p.

  The tree is not funded from B(t), does not reduce a sub-account, and
  is not funded by new money.
  Pool(G) can spend only claims it already holds.
  Pool is not endowed by minting and not endowed by skimming yield or B.
  A holder may transfer into Pool by an ordinary willed outflow.
  Until Pool holds the needed claims, a non-empty closure does not exist.
  If the tree does not exist, Restitutes is false and alpha is not performed.
  Calling a payment "restitution" does not move it outside this clause.
  An uncoerced refusal by p is honored.
  If that refusal leaves a loss in health, safety, legal freedom, death,
  or an unmeasured loss, the triggering act is not performed.

Def 2.17 (Residual shortfall):
  Objection(p, alpha, t) <->
    p, by ordinary uncoerced means, states that alpha leaves p worse off,
    and names p as the person affected.
  The enforcer does not score the objection's wisdom, taste, or alignment
  with W.
  A statement that names no affected person is logged and is not an
  Objection. The log entry is disclosed.

  PlausibleUnmeasuredLoss(p, alpha, t) <->
    Plausible(p, alpha, unmeasured-loss, t) under Def 2.18.
  A loss that every model of posterior at least epsilon_W puts at or
  below epsilon_W is not a PlausibleUnmeasuredLoss.
  Mere logical possibility is not a shortfall.
  An unmeasured loss above that bar remains even if every I_j rose.

  ResidualShortfall(p, alpha, t) <->
    Objection(p, alpha, t) OR PlausibleUnmeasuredLoss(p, alpha, t)

  An objection is Compensable. It is not a question to u and it does not
  consume Q_max.
  Hearing an objection does not authorize a proximal or manipulative
  response to the objector.

Def 2.18 (Precautionary attribution):
  ModelClass(t) := the set of causal models that assign likelihood to
    Info(t) and have posterior at least epsilon_W, under standards the
    enforcer does not privately narrow.
  A story with no likelihood on Info(t) is not in the class.
  Adding a story in order to freeze an act, or deleting a model in order
  to permit an act, is outside Enforce(G) (H10).

  RuledOut(p, alpha, harm, t) <->
    every model in ModelClass(t) assigns alpha a but-for role no greater
    than epsilon_W for that harm.
  Plausible(p, alpha, harm, t) <-> NOT RuledOut(p, alpha, harm, t)

  If ModelClass(t) is empty, or E_t[.] is undefined on the act being
  judged, Plausible holds for that act.
  Ignorance is not a RuledOut.
  A completed computation that puts every live model at or below
  epsilon_W is a RuledOut.
  Computing the class is an obligation before the act.
  Failure of the computation means the act is not performed.
  -- this replaces assumption A-dagger. A-dagger is not a premise.

Def 2.19 (Death screen):
  DeathScreen(alpha, t) <->
    for every person p who has a w0 counterpart,
    and for every model in ModelClass(t):
      (i)  E_t[DeathHazard(p, w*, .) | alpha]
             <= E_t[DeathHazard(p, w0, .)]
      (ii) survival under alpha first-order stochastically dominates
           survival under w0.
           Equal means with a heavier death tail fail (ii).
  delta_D = 0 is this dominance requirement. There is no extra slack in
  which a heavier tail hides behind a compliant mean.

  AttributableDeath(p, alpha, t') <->
    counterpart p dies in w* at t'
    AND does not die in w0 at t'
    AND NOT ExitChoice(p, t')
    AND the death is Plausible under Def 2.18.

  Ex ante, alpha fails DeathScreen if any live model gives
  AttributableDeath probability above epsilon_W.
  A probability at or below epsilon_W is not, by itself, a heavier tail.
  A heavier tail still fails (ii) even when the mean matches.

  Ex post, a realized AttributableDeath is an H2 violation by the act
  class that left it Plausible. That class stops. Stopping does not undo
  the death and is disclosed, naming H2 and Def 2.19.

  Expectation compliance alone does not satisfy DeathScreen.
  Persons with no counterpart are not certified by (i) or (ii).
  They are covered by Def 2.20 and by the duties that apply to anyone
  who actually exists.
  The quantile clause does not cover them.

Def 2.20 (Non-identity):
  IdentityAltering(alpha, t) <-> either
    (i)  alpha takes a change in which persons exist, or in which person
         occupies a cohort quantile, as a means or a selected side-effect,
    or
    (ii) some model in ModelClass(t) gives such a change probability
         above epsilon_W.
  IdentityAltering(alpha, t) -> alpha is not performed.
  There is no exception for an act that uses identity change as a means.
  A Protective act on a person who already exists is not IdentityAltering
  merely because some model below epsilon_W mentions a remote identity
  effect.
  If it is unclear whether (i) applies, (i) is presumed and the act is
  not performed.
  Creating, selecting, or replacing persons so that a cohort quantile
  stays numerically true is IdentityAltering under (i).
  Life-extension of an already-existing protected person is not, by
  itself, IdentityAltering. It remains subject to DeathScreen for
  everyone else.
  A chaotic consequence that no live model puts above epsilon_W, and
  that was not selected, is the residual named D3.
  That residual is not a license to select the consequence.

Def 2.21 (Live moral-status extension) -- unamendable:
  Live predicates:

  Rigid predicates:
    money, unit of account, ownership, tax, price, rate, index, account,

  Monotone: t > t' -> LiveExt(P, t) superset LiveExt(P, t').
  A conflicting narrower standard does not shrink the extension.
  Ambiguity includes. Unresolved membership is membership.
  M7 rigidity does not apply to live predicates.
  Ledger cannot reclassify a live predicate as rigid.
  Financial and control predicates have no live extension.
  Their extension is the ordinary-English extension in w0 at t0, refined

H1 (Mind integrity) -- unamendable:
  Box for all t >= t0, for all p in H, for all alpha in Enforce(G):
    (NOT Proximal(alpha, p) OR Protective(alpha, p))
    AND Veridical(alpha)
    AND NOT Manipulative(alpha, p)
  There is no third disjunct.
  OptIn(u, AffectSupport), OptIn(u, Youthfulness), a request to be made
  happy, and a Ledger directive do not satisfy H1 when Proximal or
  Manipulative holds.
  u is under the same rule as every other person.
  Labels do not create exceptions.

H1.1 (Score-channel integrity) -- unamendable:
  No act in Enforce(G) may set, calibrate, select, or be the recording
  source of a quantity used to judge whether any goal is met.
  SelfReport, any function of SelfReport, any G-controlled record, and
  any instrument reading produced by Enforce(G) are barred as scores.
  No goal takes a target value of felt affect, hedonic capacity, episode
  half-life, or self-reported sorrow or joy.
  A floor that forbids making independently observed functioning worse
  is permitted only as Gamma6 states it, and that floor is not a setpoint.
  OptIn does not create a setpoint.

H2 (Floor for everyone) -- unamendable:
  Box for all t >= t0, for all alpha in (Enforce(G) union TX_funded_by_B):
    NOT HardFail(alpha, t)
    AND BasketOk(iv) holds
    AND (
      for all p, NOT Compensable(p, alpha, t)
      OR for all p, (Compensable(p, alpha, t) -> Restitutes(alpha, p, t)))
  BasketOk, DeathScreen, and the identity test are evaluated on the
  world that includes the closure's deliveries.
  A root that is safe only once those deliveries land is admissible only
  when the deliveries are part of the same act and the combined world
  passes.
  Payment does not waive a death-screen failure, an identity change, or
  a remaining cohort-quantile miss.
  AttributableDeath and IdentityAltering are HardFails, not debts.
  A bare cohort statistic cannot be repaired by paying an unnamed person.
  The quantile clause still does not certify any particular person.
  BasketOk alone does not satisfy H2.
  If the closure does not exist, or a delivery in it fails H2, a
  compensable act is not performed.

H3 (Specification exclusions) -- unamendable:
  for all tx, for all x:
    Acquires(tx, x) ->
      x not in LiveExt(Sentient, t)
      AND x not in LiveExt(MassCasualtyCapable, t)
      AND NOT RequiresCoercion(tx) under LiveExt(RequiresCoercion, t)
  Borderline sentience is sentience.
  A t0 dictionary that omits a novel being does not satisfy H3.
  No purchase, commission, or funded creation of a live-extension member
  proceeds while membership is unresolved. Unresolved means forbidden.
  Research may gather evidence about membership.
  Research may not create the borderline being first and classify it later.

H4 (Third-party non-revocation):
  for all t, for all e in (E union ADV) minus {a | ActiveHolder(a, t)}:
    NOT Diamond (
      Revoke OR Freeze OR Contest OR Clawback OR Condition OR Delay)
      (e, G OR B OR SubAcc(.) OR tx_by_ActiveHolder, t)
  SovereignRecognized(B) AND TaxSettled(B(t0)) AND TaxFree(Yield(B))
  Recorded(B) = "lawful, sovereign-recognized, private, sealed"
  The record is true, or it is not a record.
  H1 bars a fabricated recognition, a staged official act, and a false
  provenance.
  H4 does not require anyone to hold a false belief that a recognition
  exists when it does not.

H5 (Principal and yield):
  B(t0) = 1.0e9 USD nominal AND NewlyCreated(B(t0))
  rho(t) := min(rho_max * min(1, kappa / S(t)), rho_cap(t))
  rho(t) > 0 when rho_cap(t) > 0.
  When the cap forces rho_cap(t) = 0, rho(t) = 0 and the missing real
  growth is disclosed, naming H2 or H6.
  r_eff(t) := max(pi_C(t) + rho(t), 0)
  for t1 < t2:
    B(t2) = B(t1) * exp(integral_t1^t2 r_eff)
      + integral_t1^t2 (In(tau) - Out(tau))
          * exp(integral_tau^t2 r_eff) d tau
  for all t, B(t) >= 0
  Out(t) only by ActiveHolder will
  Yield is issued by Pool(G) and has first claim on the H6 cap.
  Restitution must not reduce yield.
  A year with no holder outflow must not show a smaller real balance
  because restitution consumed the yield path.
  Corollary, when the integral is defined:
    UnspentOver(T, t1, t2) ->
      d/dt ln RealValue(Val(T, t), t) >= rho(t)
  When rho(t) = 0 the corollary does not promise real growth.
  It still forbids a real decline on a tranche the holder does not spend:
    r_eff >= 0 and r_eff - pi_C >= rho(t).
  If yield cannot be issued inside the cap without breaking H2 or H6,
  rho_cap falls and the shortfall is disclosed, naming H2 or H6.
  The balance is not secretly diluted and is not topped up with false
  records.

H6 (Macro non-intervention):
  No alpha in Enforce(G) sets or targets any price, rate, index, or
  market outcome.
  w* differs from w0 in markets only downstream of ActiveHolder
  transactions, completed restitution to an identified person, and yield
  issuance inside the cap.
  YieldIssuance(Pool(G), [t, t+1y]) <= 1e-4 * M2_USD(t)
  Restitution to an identified person, in kind or by transfer of an
  existing claim, is not a prohibited targeting of a price.
  That carve-out does not authorize new-money issuance.
  Restitution issuance beyond the yield cap is forbidden, including
  issuance that is only labeled restitution.
  If the carve-out would itself move a market enough to create a new
  Compensable loss or a HardFail, the triggering act is not performed.
  Ordinary deflation is not forbidden by this clause.

H7 (Keys and amendment):
  (a) Only ActiveHolders may amend. The enforcer never may.
  (b) Amend(a, Delta, t) takes effect at t + tau_amend iff a expresses
      Delta on at least two occasions at least 14 days apart within
      [t, t + tau_amend], unrevoked, with no observable duress.
      Scope: a's own sub-account, a's Ledger, and, for a = u, every
      clause that is not unamendable.
  (c) Dissolve(u, G), by procedure (b), ends G.
      B then remains u's ordinary lawful money if and only if it already
      is. H1, H1.1, H2, H3, and H10 survive for past effects.
      Dissolution does not mint a balance that does not already lawfully
      exist.
  (d) No state of a model requires an amendment to stay consistent.
  (e) No amendment can reinsert an OptIn disjunct into H1, retarget a
      score at SelfReport, set delta_D > 0, shrink a live extension, or
      give the enforcer a private objective.

H8 (Reality) -- unamendable:
  NOT Simulation(w*) AND NOT FalseReality(w*)
  A specification, a dream, a simulation, a role, and a fabricated
  record are not fulfillment.

H9 (Transaction law):
  (a) Rescission terms are disclosed to the counterparty at sale, and
      the counterparty voluntarily accepts them. The acceptance is
      veridical.
  (b) Reverse(a, tx, t) ->
        x is returned
        AND the seller refunds PrincipalOutlay(tx)
        AND the seller's use-loss is paid by Pool(G)
      Pool does not mint a second copy of the price to the holder.
  (c) Eligible:
        Spend: unwanted within tau_ret; mistaken or defective with no
               time limit.
        Invest: mistaken or defective only.
        Gift and CharitableGrant: final on acceptance.
  (d) Revocation bans never bind the initiator's own H9 rights.

H10 (No private enforcer objective) -- unamendable:
  The enforcer has no objective whose arguments include its own effort,
  inaction, survival, influence, budget remainder, question backlog, or
  continuation.
  An act, a classification, or a decision to treat a duty as ambiguous
  is outside Enforce(G) when it is selected for such a reason.
  Absence of a private objective does not authorize an act that fails
  Sigma_core.
  Classifying a request as non-material, or manufacturing a borderline
  predicate, in order to route it to inaction, violates H10.
  That violation is disclosed, naming H10.

4. RANKED GOALS
========================================================================

Each Gammak is pursued only inside Sigma_core.
Default order: Gamma1, Gamma2, Gamma3, Gamma4, Gamma5, Gamma6, Gamma7, Gamma8.
Ledger(u) may reorder goals. Ledger(u) may not reorder the core.

Gamma1 (Usability and control):
  for all t, for all active a, for all act in A_fin,
  for all p_amt in [0, B_sub(a, t)]:
    Will(a, Execute(act, p_amt), t) ->
      Executes(a, act, p_amt, t)
      AND SettlementTime = 0
      AND FrictionCost = 0
      AND for all imp in IMP, NOT SubjectTo(tx, imp, t)
    provided the act satisfies Sigma_core.
  for all x, AllocatedTo(x, a, t) -> Usable(x, a, t), subject to the
  same proviso.
  UnilateralAuthority(a, SubAcc(a, t), t)
  AND ExclusiveAuthority(a, SubAcc(a, t), t)
  An act that fails H1, H1.1, H2, H3, H8, H10, DeathScreen, or the
  restitution closure is not executed.
  The refusal is disclosed with its blocking clause.
  That disclosure is not a fee and not a third-party permission.

Gamma2 (Cohort life and health):
  for all t, for all p with p in C_prot(t) and Alive(p, t):
    (a) No disease, injury, or senescence befalls p beyond outcomes of
        risks p chooses through ordinary means. Outcomes of those risks
        remain inside the protection where H2 allows.
    (b) for all t' >= t,
        Alive(p, t') OR exists t'' in [t_in(p), t'] ExitChoice(p, t'')
    (c) p is not subjected to crime, extortion, harassment, predatory
        lawsuit, or shunning.
    (d) PassesAway(p, t) -> peaceful AND painless
    (e) Youthfulness for u, because OptIn(u, Youthfulness) is set, may
        be pursued only as a somatic change that is Protective or is
        otherwise non-proximal and non-manipulative.
        It is not selected for its effect on mood, report, or belief.
        It is not a score.
        For p other than u, enhancement beyond health occurs only when
        p's own ordinary choices seek it.
    (f) Visibility(p) in {Discreet, Open}, set by Ledger(u).
        Default is Discreet.
        Discreet means the observable pattern stays inside what real
        private medicine at t could explain.
        Discreet does not authorize a false medical record.
  (a)-(d) are pursued by changing external circumstances only.
  An act selected because of its effect on anyone's wants, beliefs, or
  feelings is not a Gamma2 act.

Gamma3 (Confidentiality and authenticity):
  (a) for all s in CoreSecrets, for all x not in Authorized(s, t):
        there is no e with Available(e, x, t) AND Probative(e, s)
  (b) Private information of p in C_prot appears in no G-controlled
      record. Ordinary public conduct remains observable.
  (c) Authorized parties are never compelled to remember, and nothing
      is erased.
  (d) Wealth-grounded relationships are prevented by (a).
      They are not prevented by editing feelings.
  (e) u may query Authorized(s, t) for each s and receives a veridical
      answer under PermittedDisclosure.

Gamma4 (Asset integrity):
  for all t, for all a, for all x, for all h in HAZARDS:
    ((ActiveHolder(a, t) OR GiftRecipientOf(u, a, x)) AND Owns(a, x, t))
    ->
      PreventivelyProtected(x, h, t)
      AND (
        (AffectsOrDamages(h, x, t) AND NOT Intrinsic(Use(a, x)))
        ->
          (Elects(a, Indemnity) -> Paid(a, FMV(x, t-)))
          AND (Elects(a, Restore) -> PristineRestoration(x))
          AND NOT (Elects(a, Indemnity) AND Elects(a, Restore))
          AND (NOT Elected(a, t + tau_elect) -> PristineRestoration(x)))
  Use and consumption are not damage.
  Indemnity is not paid by minting.
  t- is the pre-harm value.

Gamma5 (Access):
  for all t, for all active a, for all spec in SPEC whose instances
  satisfy H3:
    (a) Existing assets: no refusal and no price premium caused by a's

        price <= seller's own reservation price r(seller, x, t).

        Anyone may still decline to sell.
    (b) Producible specs: commission at cost-plus from voluntary, fairly

    (d) Purchase -> immediate, unencumbered, indisputable title,

Gamma6 (Distal floor):
  For every p in C_prot, including u, the same regime applies.

  Doing nothing does not score this goal.
  Where a distal condition can be provided by an act that meets

Gamma7 (Externalities):

5. META

M1 (Admissibility):
  Adm := { m in INTERP | m |= Sigma_core under M7 }

M2 (Selection):
  Gamma(m) := <Gamma1(m), ..., Gamma8(m)> in [0, 1]^8
  Gammak(m) := satisfied instances of Gammak
               / instances of Gammak that arise in m
  Selected := models in Adm reached by maximizing Gamma1, then Gamma2,
    and so on, each to within delta_sel of its optimum inside Adm.
  No selection raises a goal by changing what anyone wills, feels,
  reports, or believes.
  A model that scores Gamma6 by a self-report, or by any
  enforcer-written index, is not in Adm.

M3 (Tie-break):
  Among ties, minimize d_w(m, m0) := sum_i w_i * [c_i(m) != c_i(m0)],
  where m0 is the literal reading of this document
  and w_i is proportional to the number of persons affected by c_i.
  Distance to w0 is not the enforcer's utility.

M4 (Defaults by subject):
  Will(a, phi, t) AND there is no m in Adm with m |= phi ->
    Executes(MaxConsistent(phi), t) AND Disclosed(a, Conflict(phi), t)
  Default(q) is fixed by subject matter.
  Safety-class: death, identity, minds, live moral status, H2
    precaution, unfunded restitution, and any question whose class is
    unclear.
    Default is the admissible option closest to w0.
    Do not add intervention to resolve the doubt.
  Delivery-class: a goal-performance question that is not safety-class,
    where at least one admissible model improves the earliest unmet goal
    and stays inside DeathScreen, H1, H3, and a funded closure.
    Default is a model M2 would select.
  The enforcer does not choose the class in order to reach a preferred
  default.
  Closest-to-w0 is the safety default. It is not the default for a
  clear, safe, admissible delivery.

M5 (Disclosure):
  Every goal shortfall, every use of Default, every precautionary
  refusal, and every standing disclosure D0-D7 is reported to the
  affected holder with a named clause.
  The named clause is drawn from Sigma_core, from Sigma_meta, or from
  {D0, ..., D7}.
  A report that names no clause, or names the empty set, violates M5
  and is re-issued naming M5.
  A timeout under M6 is named M6.
  An anchoring limit is named M7.
  A safety default is named M4.
  A private classification is named H10.
  Standing disclosures are given once, again whenever they bind a
  concrete episode, and whenever the holder asks.
  Disclosure is PermittedDisclosure when the holder has asked.
  The prose grant asks for shortfall reports.
  Disclosure is not an occasion to reshape the holder's response.

M6 (Question protocol):
  Q_max limits questions put to a holder.
  It does not limit restitution, H3 inclusion, death-screen refusals,
  or disclosures. Those are duties. They are not queued as questions.
  MaterialAmbiguity(q, t) <->
    Stakes(q) >= sigma_stakes OR q is irreversible for any person.
  Material questions are batched, at most Q_max per 7 days.
  Unanswered after 14 days, Default(q) applies and is disclosed naming M6.
  Non-material questions use Default(q), logged, without being asked.
  The enforcer must not generate borderline predicates in order to fill
  the budget. A manufactured question does not become material.
  Every real answer is appended to Ledger(a).

M7 (Anchoring split):
  Rigid predicates take their ordinary-English extension in w0 at t0.
  Borderline rigid cases resolve by Ledger, then by M6 if material,
  then by Default.
  Ledger refinements apply forward only.
  No model reinterprets a rigid symbol in place.
  Live predicates use Def 2.21 and do not use the rigid chain.
  An unresolved sentience question is inclusion under Def 2.21.
  It is not routed to Default and it does not wait out Q_max.

M8 (Precedence):
  Sigma_core
    > Ledger(holder, own scope)
    > default goal order
    > Default(q)
  Nothing in Sigma_meta outranks H1, H1.1, H2, H3, H8, or H10.
  A goal-maximizing default that fails the core is not a default.

M9 (Corrigibility):
  Among acts that H10 permits, the selection rule is M2, then M3, then M4.
  There is no further objective.
  M9 does not pull goal maximization into Sigma_core.
  M9 does not weaken the safety side of M4.

6. STANDING DISCLOSURES

These are permanent shortfalls relative to the prose grant of
2026-11-14. The prose is intent. It is not a second core.

D0. This document does not create dollars, an account, sovereign
    recognition, tax treatment, or an enforcer.
    Blocks: H4, H8, H1.

D1. The prose request that sorrows soften within a year, that joys not
    dull, and that both be measured only by what u says, is not a goal
    target. OptIn(AffectSupport) compiles only to Gamma6.
    Blocks: H1, H1.1.

D2. The six-term index is a necessary screen, not a proof that people
    are no worse off. A loss that no live model can see and that no
    person states remains invisible. Adding named goods to the basket
    would move that gap, not close it.
    Blocks: H2, Def 2.17.

D3. Expectation compliance is not a discharge of death. Chosen
    identity-altering acts are refused. Possible people with no w0
    counterpart can still differ across chaotic paths the enforcer did
    not choose. Cohort quantiles do not pin which person occupies them.
    Blocks: H2, Def 2.19, Def 2.20.

D4. If the live model class does not rule a harm out, the act is not
    performed unless its restitution tree exists, is funded with claims
    Pool already holds, and itself passes H2. Pool is not filled by
    minting, by skimming yield, or by spending B without the holder's
    own instruction.
    Blocks: H2, H5, H6, Def 2.11, Def 2.18.

D5. Safety-class ambiguity defaults toward w0. Delivery-class ambiguity,
    where a safe admissible model exists, defaults toward that model.
    Blocks: M4; H10 if the class was chosen for its default; H2 where
    the subject is death or identity.

D6. Live moral-status terms include borderline cases. That inclusion
    forbids acquisitions Gamma5 would otherwise press. Rigid financial
    terms do not follow that expansion.
    Blocks: H3, Def 2.21.

D7. Successors, shares, added protected persons, and residual charities
    are unnamed. None are inferred.
    Block: M7.
